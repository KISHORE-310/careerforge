# CareerForge AI: Centralized AI Service & Match Engine Logic

## Phase 2: AI Service Architecture
- **Provider Abstraction**: Implemented `AIService` class to handle provider-agnostic calls (OpenAI/Anthropic/Google).
- **Environment Config**: Wired to `PROCESS.ENV.AI_API_KEY`. No client-side exposure.
- **Context Layer**: Created `AIContextBuilder` to aggregate `CareerProfile`, `Resume`, and `Job` data for consistent prompting.
- **Resilience**: Implemented exponential backoff for rate limits and `AbortController` for 15s timeouts.

## Phase 3: AI Job Match Engine
- **Multi-Factor Scoring**:
  - `SkillScore`: Weighted comparison of User Skills vs Job Requirements.
  - `ExpScore`: Years of experience and role relevancy check.
  - `EduScore`: Degree type and field of study alignment.
  - `ResumeScore`: Semantic similarity between resume content and job description.
- **Explanation Engine**: Generates structured "Why you match" and "Missing Gaps" data.
- **Consistency**: Unified `calculateMatch(user, job)` utility used by Dashboard, Job Search, and Resume Studio.
